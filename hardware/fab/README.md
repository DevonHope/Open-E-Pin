# Open E-Pin v0.2: fabrication and assembly notes

Written for the board in `../kicad/open-e-pin.kicad_pcb`. The files here are generated by
`../scripts/gen_fab.sh`; regenerate them after any board change.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `open-e-pin-gerbers.zip` | Everything in `gerber/` in one file for upload |
| `gerber/` | Gerber X2 for all copper, mask, paste, silkscreen and outline layers; Excellon drill files (plated and non-plated separate) with drill maps |
| `assembly/open-e-pin-pos.csv` | Pick-and-place, back side (every part is on the back) |
| `assembly/open-e-pin-bom.csv` | Bill of materials with manufacturer part numbers |

## Board

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Layers | 4: F.Cu (signals, GND pour under the display), In1.Cu GND plane, In2.Cu 3V0 plane, B.Cu (parts, signals, GND pour) |
| Finished thickness | 0.8 mm |
| Copper | 1 oz outer, 0.5 oz inner |
| Material | FR-4, Tg 150 or better |
| Outline | 56 mm round with a flat and two snap-hook notches; two 4.6 mm non-plated holes |
| Minimum track / space | 0.15 mm / 0.15 mm (0.127 mm allowed in the rules, not used) |
| Vias | 0.5 mm pad, 0.25 mm drill, tented both sides. No via in any SMD land |
| Solder mask | Both sides; minimum mask dam 0.1 mm (between the 0.4 mm-pitch WCSP lands) |
| Surface finish | ENIG (flat lands for the 0.4 mm-pitch WCSP and the 0.5 mm FPC connector) |
| Class | IPC-6012 Class 2 |
| Electrical test | 100 % netlist test |

## Assembly

- **One side only:** all parts are on the back (B.Cu); one reflow pass.
- **Fiducials:** FID1 to FID3, 1 mm copper, 2 mm mask opening, on the back.
- **Panel:** the board is round, so assemble it in a panel with 5 mm rails on two sides, tooling
  holes in the rails and tab-routed breakaways. Keep tabs away from J1 (USB-C), SW1 (side button),
  the module antenna end (U1, left edge seen from the back) and the display flat.
- **Fine pitch:** U2 (BQ25101YFPR) and U4 (TPS22916CYFPR) are 0.4 mm-pitch WCSP with NSMD lands
  (0.23 mm copper, 0.30 mm mask opening, 0.25 mm paste aperture). Use a 0.08 mm stencil (paste
  area ratio 0.78, IPC-7525), or a 0.10 mm stencil stepped down to 0.08 mm round U2/U4. At JLCPCB
  this needs Standard PCBA, not Economic (minimum BGA pitch 0.5 mm there).
- **J3** (Hirose FH12-32S-0.5SH(55)) is 0.5 mm pitch; inspect for bridges.
- **Customer-supplied parts:** U1 (Raytac MDBT50Q-1MV2) is rarely in distributor stock; plan to
  consign it.
- **Not fitted:** TP1 to TP8 are bare pads.
- **Moisture sensitivity:** bake per J-STD-033 if the module or WCSP reels have been open longer
  than their MSL floor life.
- **Acceptance:** IPC-A-610 Class 2.

## Known DRC items (accepted)

- 4 hole-clearance items inside KiCad's GCT USB4105 footprint: the receptacle's plastic locating
  pegs sit 0.19 mm from the A1/A12 and B1/B12 pads, as in GCT's recommended layout.
- Silkscreen clipped by solder mask or overlapping: cosmetic.
